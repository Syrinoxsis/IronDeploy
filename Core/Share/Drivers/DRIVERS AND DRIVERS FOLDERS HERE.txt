Place extracted, signed Windows driver packages and driver folders here.

Subdirectories are supported. IronDeploy recursively finds all .inf files
below Z:\Drivers and stages their packages in the offline Windows DriverStore
before the first boot. Windows Plug and Play installs only packages compatible
with the target hardware. Keep companion and software-component INF packages
together with their primary driver because modern audio and display drivers
often depend on them.

Executable installers (.exe and .msi) are not processed.
